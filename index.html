Career

2012 – present

Most people in GRC come from one direction. I came from two. IT operations taught me how things actually break. Years of corporate training taught me how to write and present in a way that gets people to act on what they read. In compliance, that second part is underrated. The best risk register in the world does nothing if nobody acts upon it. I am now putting both to work in the German market, building on ISO 27001 and NIS2, studying toward TestDaF C1 German, and completing my MSc in Computer Science at Heriot-Watt University.
